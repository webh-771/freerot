He Chose His Adopted Sister, So I Chose Another Man

20 Episodes

Blair Bennett is in bed with her husband, Ethan Whitmore, when his adopted sister, Lily, barges in wearing a nearly transparent nightgown. Lily takes over their home, crosses every boundary, and openly provokes Blair—yet Ethan keeps choosing Lily. When Lily smashes a vase against Blair’s head and stages a crisis, Ethan carries Lily away without looking back. Blair stops fighting for her marriage. She files for divorce and chooses the one man Ethan never saw coming: her executive assistant, Mason.At Whitmore Capital’s Manhattan charity gala, Blair puts the evidence of Ethan and Lily’s betrayal on the ballroom screen, then reveals she is pregnant by another man. Ethan confesses, fights the settlement, and tries to rebuild the home he destroyed, but Blair refuses to return. Then Mason comes back as Mason Reed, the hidden youngest heir of a powerful Boston family…
