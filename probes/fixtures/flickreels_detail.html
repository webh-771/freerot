She Made Him Lose Everything

Total: 40 Eps Server: Primary

Mia, president of Global Holdings, once truly loved her husband, Smith. She single-handedly raised him from nothing into a Wall Street financial giant. But she gradually discovered that he had cheated with a female student she sponsored, poisoned her over a long period, transferred company equity, and even celebrated over her corpse. Having survived, Mia fully awakened and fought back with the law, capital, and strategy. She reclaimed the company, exposed the truth, and revealed Smith's greed and hypocrisy to the world. Fallen from the peak to rock bottom, Smith finally understood: whoever can put him on a pedestal can also leave him with nothing overnight.
